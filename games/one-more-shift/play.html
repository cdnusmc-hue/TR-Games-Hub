HOW TO PLAY

Keep the gadgets moving.

Your goal: deliver the three 16-gadget orders before their deadlines, while keeping your 18 crew healthy. You have 45 shift minutes. Extra rush work is optional.

01Read the flow.Gather prepares parts → Assemble builds gadgets → Dispatch delivers them. Crew work automatically while the clock runs.
02Move people.Click an area’s Select crew, choose 1 or 2 ordinary in YOUR MOVE, then click Send selected here at the destination. Transfers take one shift minute. Click a person to select them; Shift-click adds others.
03Watch, pause, adapt.A growing queue means work is waiting at that station. An empty queue means it needs supplies from upstream. Pause freely to inspect and change your plan.
Queues, products and processing speeds

The big numbers are items waiting to be processed at each station, not completed orders. Delivered items fill the order cards above the workshop.

All named products use the same steps and speeds in this prototype. The differences are order quantity and deadline. Each fully rested worker processes 0.25 items/min at Gather, 0.20 at Assemble, and 0.32 at Dispatch. Manual assembly processes 0.10 items/min per worker.

The displayed capacity is your current crew’s combined potential per shift minute. Actual output also needs queued items and a working machine. Capacity is lower when people are tired. Limits: Gather 8, Assemble 8, Dispatch 6, Manual 4 crew.

Machine failure and technicians

The assembly machine fails at minute 8, with a warning at minute 6. Pause and choose your recovery.

Repair: click Select technicians in the Repair bay, then Send selected there. Both technicians must be present for four shift minutes of repair, plus travel. After repair, select them again and send them back to productive work.

Manual backup: while the machine is broken, send up to four people, including at least one technician, to Manual assembly. It is slower and uses twice as much energy. Using a technician here means both cannot repair together.

Energy, priorities and the rush offer

Energy bars show individual condition. Below 40 energy, people work at 75% speed. Below 20, they automatically travel to rest; they become available again at 40. Send selected crew to the Break area to recover 4 energy per shift minute, then move them back yourself.

Orders normally receive gadgets by the earliest open deadline. Prioritize directs the next deliveries to the chosen order; it does not change production speed.

At minute 10 the game pauses for a rush offer: accept 0, 6 or 12 extra gadgets, arriving at minute 12 and due at minute 42. Passing has no hidden penalty. Resume after deciding.

Normal pace: about 6 real minutes per shift. Slower: about 12. Unlimited pause; no penalty. Your finish shows on-time deliveries and crew condition separately. Replay to try another plan.
