# TR Games Hub philosophy

## 1. Product philosophy and design principles
1. **Pleasure before instruction.** A game must work when its learning claims are removed. Movement, sound, anticipation, surprise, and recovery must be satisfying.
2. **Choices have opportunity costs.** Offer at least two viable responses to important situations. Avoid a dominant universal action or hidden moral answer key.
3. **Consequences are legible.** Show queues growing, work moving, people tiring, and machines recovering. Let the player trace effects back to choices.
4. **People have agency.** Crew communicate limits and preferences; households have negotiated priorities; opponents have understandable interests. People are not disposable efficiency tokens.
5. **Difficulty adds relationships.** Introduce dependencies, uncertainty, timing, and competing goals before increasing raw speed. Pause and accessible pacing remain available.
6. **Capability is progression.** Unlock new approaches, responsibilities, environments, and combinations. Currency can support fiction but cannot substitute for mastery.
7. **Recovery is play.** A setback creates a new decision, not an unavoidable spiral. Rematch should test a revised strategy.
8. **Reflection is optional and concrete.** A short replay and one causal observation beat a lecture. Never claim that a game score diagnoses real-world competence.
9. **Respect attention and money.** Prefer paid games, free demos, and substantial optional expansions. No paid recovery, energy gates, loot boxes, streak coercion, artificial scarcity, or child-targeted purchasing pressure.
10. **Small first, shared later.** Prove a loop before building a universal engine, account system, multiplayer, or portfolio portal.

Human judgment is exercised when players compare alternatives, commit under uncertainty, observe a result, and revise a strategy. The game may express humane operating constraints without prescribing a correct economic, political, or personal worldview. Show the assumptions behind modeled outcomes; distinguish facts in the simulation from authorial interpretation.
