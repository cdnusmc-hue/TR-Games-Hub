## 2. Portfolio opportunity briefs
The user supplied titles and the One More Shift scenario. All other premises below are proposed interpretations, not recovered prior designs. Ages indicate design targets, not formal ratings. Complexity is relative to a small single-player release: Low, Medium, High. All concepts initially validate through a desktop browser build; platform entries describe plausible release directions.

## 3. Families, overlaps and reusable foundations
Primary families above organize investment; a game can exercise skills from several families. The Negotiation stands alone as an interpersonal prototype because dialogue systems and writing require distinct validation.

| Shared foundation | Candidates | Reuse after validation | Keep distinct |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow and allocation simulation | One More Shift, The Bottleneck, Tiny Factory, Last Mile | Fixed-step simulation, queues, transfers, capacity, event timeline, seeded runs, replay log | Factory construction and city routing are substantial separate systems |
| Turn/event resource model | Payday to Payday, Gig City, Market Day, Household Together, Career Moves | Time/money/energy resources, event requirements, visible tradeoffs, save format | Household agency, route maps and narrative branches |
| Evidence and intervention cases | Fix the Fix, Proof Positive, Tiny Town Detective, The Repair Shop | Evidence provenance, hypotheses, test actions, authored causal graph, spoiler-safe results | Physical diagnosis versus disputed testimony |
| Spatial constraints | The Floor Plan, Build It Twice, Numbers & Neighbors | Grid tools, constraint checks and scenario authoring | Physics, room flow and number rules |
| Musical sequencing | Make the Hit, The Setlist | Stem playback, arrangement data, crowd response model | Composition and live pacing |
| Tactical crew and contingency | Trash Panda Heist, One More Shift, The Last 20 Minutes | Crew selection, task intent, readable forecast, deterministic replay | Stealth perception and narrative rescue logic |

**Merge:** The Repair Shop becomes the world for Fix the Fix; Tiny Town Detective becomes the world/case series for Proof Positive. Market Day becomes the concrete first version of Side Hustle. The Setlist becomes a module and potential small test within Make the Hit. The Last 20 Minutes supplies scenario pacing to One More Shift. Last Mile becomes a future route-focused mode for Gig City, subject to its own fun test.

**Reshape:** Household CEO → Household Together, with negotiated needs and shared agency. Plan B → a recovery design pattern across the portfolio until it has a distinctive fantasy. The Bottleneck → a calm puzzle mode/test harness associated with One More Shift; preserve it as a separate product option if players prefer deliberation to time pressure.

**Park standalone production:** Tiny Factory, Build It Twice, Creature Garden, Career Moves, The Negotiation, The Floor Plan, Numbers & Neighbors and Gig City. Preserve their briefs; do not commission full GDDs. Their scores or complexity do not justify opening more production fronts now.

Do not force shared code because titles share a theme. First build only the lead's required primitives. Extract stable reuse when a second playable game demonstrates it.

### Test the lead assumption
One More Shift scores 91, Trash Panda Heist 90. That is not decisive evidence. Equal weighting would tie them; shifting five percentage points from mission alignment to differentiation would put Trash Panda Heist ahead. The lead recommendation therefore rests on a practical judgment: One More Shift's flow model can expose its core decision loop without stealth AI, large maps, or extensive authored content. Operations experience can help produce believable tensions, but non-operations players must determine whether they are fun.

The Bottleneck and Payday to Payday tie at 86. The Bottleneck is the quickest fallback if real-time pressure is the problem. Payday is the more useful independent secondary because it diversifies the portfolio and connects to Runway. Fix the Fix scores above Make the Hit; music is still worth incubation because it tests creative judgment and offers a different audience, not because it is production-ready.

**Kill or pivot the lead if:** players mainly watch queues; the best policy is always maximum staffing at one station; players need a verbal explanation to make ordinary decisions; or a second run feels like repeating chores. Reskinning alone does not fix an uninteresting loop. Test a calm Bottleneck variant when time pressure obscures good decisions; test a small Trash Panda Heist paper scenario if the workplace fantasy itself is the problem.
## 6. Connections to Thinking Reimagined
| Connection | Strategic value | Boundary and next evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Payday to Payday → Runway | A fictional pay-cycle game can make cash timing and reserves intuitive, then offer an optional invitation to the practical budgeting tool | Separate fiction from financial advice; no personal-account import or compulsory funnel. Validate game enjoyment before referral value |
| Fix the Fix → RCA | Players test competing causes and observe whether interventions prevent recurrence | Causal case graph must support alternative tests. Do not force a five-why worksheet or one prescribed reasoning sequence |
| Proof Positive → evidence reasoning / Genesis principles | Players weigh source reliability, seek contradictory clues and calibrate confidence | Multiple hypotheses can remain unresolved. Do not equate institutional status with automatic truth or impose a worldview |
| One More Shift + The Bottleneck → systems thinking | Experience capacity constraints, delayed effects, upstream/downstream dependencies and recovery | State what the simplified model assumes; real operations and people are more complex |
| Games Hub → wider TR | A distinct entertainment division demonstrates the mission through voluntary play | Separate product backlog and assets from SPY research, Runway and breathing apps. Shared principles do not imply shared data or production pipelines |

Games are an adjacent portfolio lane, not a reason to reopen all TR projects at once. Runway can continue its own completion lane; games receive one bounded prototype experiment. SPY remains an independent research project and does not supply a trading theme or monetization assumption here.
